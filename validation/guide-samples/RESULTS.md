# EMANE Guide sample validation

Date: 2026-08-28

Rust base commit: `09eeae5c653c6f1e2e7fd2052c437e9d50689fc2`

The fixes described below are intentionally uncommitted.

Guide commit: `b02f64fdfd10908ca3f13d65f17b9dbe7bcc6581`

Reference: [The EMANE Guide](https://emane.io/)

These fixtures reduce the guide experiments to the minimum NEM count needed
to exercise the same radio-model behavior. They preserve the guide's PHY, MAC,
PCR, pathloss, and TDMA schedule values. Results are compared with the
published guide outputs; a legacy C++ binary was not installed locally for a
second live run.

## RF Pipe

Result: PASS

- 70 dB pathloss produced -70.0 dBm receive power, -96.989700043 dBm
  noise floor, and 26.989700043 dB SINR.
- Baseline ping delivered 200/200 packets.
- 86.98 dB pathloss produced -86.980003357 dBm receive power and
  10.009696686 dB SINR after clearing the cumulative receive-metric table.
- The guide predicts approximately 25% bidirectional ping completion at this
  point. The Rust run delivered 247/1000 (24.7%); the guide's representative
  run delivered 234/1000 (23.4%).

## IEEE 802.11abg

Result: PASS

- 70 dB links produced -70.0 dBm receive power and 26.989700043 dB SINR.
- The 75 dB NEM 1-to-3 link produced -75.0 dBm receive power and
  21.989700043 dB SINR.
- The baseline NEM 1-to-3 link delivered 10/10 pings at the guide's configured
  54 Mbit/s data rate.
- A continuous 2.39 GHz, 20 MHz, -20 dBm jammer was generated by a separate
  Rust NEM and received at -90 dBm after the guide's 70 dB pathloss. Its
  half-channel overlap raised the observed noise floor to approximately
  -93 dBm, matching the guide's approximately 4 dB SINR reduction.
- Under the jammer, the 75 dB link delivered 0/10 pings at 54 Mbit/s, matching
  the guide's near-zero PCR at approximately 18 dB SINR.
- The guide's runtime control-port change to rate index 10 was applied to NEMs
  1 and 3. At 36 Mbit/s, the same jammed link recovered to 10/10 pings.
- The initial reduced fixture contained only the 54 Mbit/s PCR curve. It was
  replaced with all 12 curves from the guide, and a regression test verifies
  that every rate is available.

## TDMA

Result: PASS after fixing the Rust TDMA transmit sub-ID

- Both NEMs accepted the guide's exact schedule with no rejection.
- `scheduler.ScheduleInfoTable` and `scheduler.StructureInfoTable` matched the
  guide: 10 slots, the expected TX/RX assignments, 1.2 GHz, 9 Mbit/s, 50 dBm,
  5 MHz bandwidth, 10 ms slot duration, and 640 us overhead.
- Before the fix, transmitted frames reached the receiving PHY at -30 dBm but
  no frame reached the TDMA MAC. TDMA was hard-coding `TxProperties.sub_id` to
  1 while the guide configures PHY sub-ID 7.
- TDMA now sends sub-ID 0, which tells the PHY to substitute its configured
  sub-ID, matching the other Rust MAC plugins and the core PHY contract.
- With the unmodified guide sub-ID 7 fixture, the fixed runtime delivered
  20/20 pings. NEMs 1 and 2 recorded 24 and 23 valid RX slots respectively,
  and both reported nonzero upstream packet counts with zero schedule
  rejections.
- Receive power remained -30 dBm, as expected from 50 dBm transmit power and
  80 dB pathloss.

## Bent Pipe 01

Result: PASS

- The direct two-NEM fixture delivered 20/20 pings with 87 dB pathloss.
- Receive SINR was 1.7390874094431865 dB and the noise floor was
  -88.23908740944319 dBm.
- Antenna, 150 MHz channel, 29.91 GHz frequency, 100 Mbit/s data rate, and
  2048-byte MTU status matched the guide.

## Bent Pipe 02

Result: PASS after three Rust compatibility fixes

- Guide antenna profile and manifest XML files include DTD declarations.
  Rust now permits those declarations while parsing the guide-shaped files.
- U-bend transponders do not classify locally originated traffic, so the guide
  omits `transponder.transmit.tosmap` for them. Rust now requires that field
  only for process transponders.
- Rust previously used modeled propagation delay for collision/PCR timing but
  delivered and relayed packets immediately. Reception and U-bend relay are
  now scheduled after modeled reception completion.
- The exact three-NEM satellite fixture delivered 5/5 pings with RTT
  482.904/580.406/968.454/194.024 ms. The guide reports
  482.364/580.068/966.982/193.458 ms.
- All four reported link SINRs matched the guide exactly: 11.29777283855799,
  8.066688845808386, 8.137849777137916, and 11.390794198620853 dB.

## Comm Effect 01

Result: PASS

- All links from NEM 1 to NEMs 2 through 5 delivered with zero loss under the
  guide's baseline 1 Mbit/s unicast and broadcast settings.
- Publishing the guide's 0.1 second latency update kept zero loss and produced
  200.775 to 201.290 ms round-trip times, matching the guide's 201 to 202 ms.

## Rust test suite

`cargo test --workspace`: 114 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored. The ignored raw
transport test requires a dedicated privileged veth fixture.

Regression coverage verifies TDMA sub-ID delegation, guide-shaped antenna XML
with DTDs, U-bend transponder field requirements, and all IEEE guide PCR rates.

All temporary EMANE processes, TAP interfaces, and network namespaces were
removed after validation.
